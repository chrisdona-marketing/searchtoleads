CHOOSING THE RIGHT MODEL

Fractional vs Outsourced vs Full-Time Marketing Manager

The right structure depends on the level of leadership, availability and internal capacity your business needs.

FLEXIBLE LEADERSHIP

Fractional Marketing Manager

Best when you need experienced marketing leadership for a defined portion of senior management capacity.

  • Senior direction
  • Flexible monthly commitment
  • Strategy and oversight
  • Works with existing resources
EXTERNAL MANAGEMENT

Outsourced Marketing Manager

Best when you need someone outside the business to take stronger ownership of marketing coordination.

  • External marketing management
  • Supplier coordination
  • Campaign oversight
  • Reporting and accountability
INTERNAL ROLE

Full-Time Marketing Manager

Best when marketing activity and team size justify a permanent senior role embedded in the organisation.

  • Full-time availability
  • Permanent internal employee
  • Higher fixed commitment
  • Suitable for larger marketing functions

Fractional Marketing Manager Dubai FAQs

Common questions about using fractional or outsourced marketing leadership for your business.

What is a fractional marketing manager?

A fractional marketing manager provides senior marketing leadership to a business on a flexible part-time basis. This can include strategy, planning, campaign oversight, budgeting, team coordination, agency management and performance reporting.

What is an outsourced marketing manager?

An outsourced marketing manager takes responsibility for coordinating and managing marketing without being a full-time internal employee. The role can connect business objectives with internal teams, agencies, freelancers and specialist resources.

How much does a fractional marketing manager cost in Dubai?

Can you work with our existing marketing team?

Yes. SearchToLeads can provide strategic direction and management while coordinating with an existing internal marketing team, sales team, agency, freelancer or specialist supplier.

Can you manage our agencies and freelancers?

Yes. Agency and freelancer coordination can form part of the outsourced marketing manager role, helping ensure external suppliers work toward agreed priorities and business objectives.

Is fractional marketing management suitable for SMEs?
