FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Brand Awareness & Social Media Marketing Dubai FAQs

Common questions about brand-building campaigns, social strategy and paid awareness support.

What is brand awareness marketing?

Brand awareness marketing focuses on increasing relevant recognition, reach and familiarity among the audiences most likely to matter to the business. It can use paid social, organic social, content, video, display or other awareness channels depending on the objective.

What does social media marketing support include?

Support can include platform and audience strategy, content pillars, campaign planning, creative direction, paid social campaigns, remarketing recommendations, KPI selection and performance review. The exact mix depends on the brand, audience and available resources.

Which social platforms can be included?

The platform mix is selected according to where the target audience is most relevant. This may include Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube or other appropriate channels rather than trying to be equally active everywhere.

Do you create all social media content?

SearchToLeads can provide strategy, content direction, campaign briefs and coordination. When additional design, video, photography or specialist production is required, delivery can scale through trusted specialist resources or the client’s existing team.

Can brand-awareness campaigns also generate leads?

They can support lead generation, but awareness and direct-response campaigns have different roles. The strategy should define the objective clearly and use the right measurement rather than judging every awareness campaign only by immediate leads.

Do you run paid awareness campaigns?

Yes. Paid social and awareness campaign support can include audience targeting, campaign structure, creative direction, testing, optimisation and performance review. Media spend is separate from service fees.

How is brand awareness measured?

Measurement can include reach, frequency, video views, engagement, profile visits, website visits, branded search, audience growth and downstream enquiry signals, depending on the channels and tracking available.

How much does brand awareness and social media marketing cost?

Pricing is scoped according to the number of platforms, campaign complexity, content and creative requirements, paid media involvement, markets and reporting needs. Advertising media spend and specialist production are separate.
