Frequently Asked Questions

Marketing Consultant & Outsourced Marketing FAQs

Common questions about marketing consulting, fractional marketing management, outsourced marketing, SEO and AI-search optimisation in the UAE.

What does a marketing consultant in Dubai do?

A marketing consultant helps a business assess its market position, marketing channels, customer acquisition activity and performance, then develops a strategy to improve visibility, lead generation and marketing efficiency. Support may include strategy, digital channels, team and agency coordination, analytics and execution oversight.

What is a Fractional Marketing Manager?

A Fractional Marketing Manager provides senior marketing leadership on a flexible basis. The role can include strategy, budgeting, channel planning, agency management, team coordination, campaign oversight and performance reporting without requiring a business to immediately hire a full-time senior Marketing Manager.

What is an outsourced marketing manager?

An outsourced marketing manager acts as an external marketing lead for the business. The role can coordinate internal staff, agencies, freelancers and specialist partners while providing strategy, planning, performance oversight and implementation support.

Is an outsourced marketing manager different from a marketing agency?

The models are different. An agency typically delivers specific marketing services or channels, while an outsourced or fractional marketing manager can work closer to the business as a strategic marketing lead, coordinating multiple channels, internal resources, agencies and specialists around the overall marketing plan.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on improving visibility in traditional search engines. AEO focuses on helping answer engines understand and surface useful answers. GEO focuses on strengthening how a brand, expertise and content can be discovered and interpreted by generative AI search systems.

Does AEO or GEO replace traditional SEO?

No. Technical SEO, useful content, search intent, site architecture, internal linking and authority remain important foundations. AEO and GEO extend those principles to answer-based and generative-search experiences.

Is outsourced marketing suitable for SMEs in Dubai?

It can be particularly useful for SMEs that need experienced marketing direction but do not yet require a large full-time internal marketing department. The business can access strategic leadership while continuing to use internal staff, agencies, freelancers and specialist resources where required.

Can SearchToLeads support both strategy and execution?

Yes. SearchToLeads combines strategic marketing direction with practical digital marketing expertise. Depending on the engagement, support can include strategy, implementation guidance, performance analysis and coordination with internal teams, agencies or specialist partners.
