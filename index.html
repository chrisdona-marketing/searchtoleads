FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Digital Marketing Consultant Dubai FAQs

Answers to common questions about working with SearchToLeads.

What does a digital marketing consultant in Dubai do?

A digital marketing consultant helps businesses decide where to focus their marketing, which channels to prioritise, how campaigns should be managed and how marketing performance connects to business growth and lead generation.

What is a fractional marketing manager?

A fractional marketing manager provides senior marketing leadership on a flexible part-time or outsourced basis. This can include strategy, campaign oversight, team coordination, agency management, budgeting and performance reporting.

Do you provide SEO, AEO and GEO services in Dubai?

Yes. SearchToLeads supports SEO together with Answer Engine Optimization and Generative Engine Optimization to improve visibility across traditional search engines and emerging AI-driven discovery environments.

Can SearchToLeads work with an existing marketing team or agency?

Yes. SearchToLeads can work alongside internal marketing teams, agencies, freelancers and specialist partners, providing strategic direction, coordination, oversight and reporting.

How much do digital marketing services cost?

SearchToLeads currently offers introductory packages starting from AED 750 for a Marketing Growth Audit Lite, AED 4,500 per month for fractional or outsourced marketing management, AED 3,500 per month for SME growth marketing, AED 2,500 per month for SEO, AEO and GEO, and AED 2,000 per month plus media spend for performance marketing.

Do you personally execute every marketing task?

SearchToLeads is consultant-led. Chris provides marketing strategy, direction and oversight, while specialist execution can be coordinated through trusted resources or partners where additional expertise or delivery capacity is required.
