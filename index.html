Frequently Asked Questions

Digital Marketing Consultant Dubai FAQs

Common questions about digital marketing consulting, fractional marketing, outsourced marketing, SEO and AI-search optimisation.

What does a digital marketing consultant in Dubai do?

A digital marketing consultant helps businesses improve marketing strategy and execution across areas such as SEO, paid advertising, content, websites, analytics and lead generation. The role can include strategy, channel planning, performance analysis and coordination with internal or external marketing resources.

Do you offer freelance digital marketing consulting in Dubai?

SearchToLeads provides independent digital marketing consulting. Support can be structured as a specific project, strategic advisory, outsourced marketing management or ongoing fractional marketing leadership depending on what the business requires.

What is a Fractional Marketing Manager?

A Fractional Marketing Manager provides experienced marketing leadership on a flexible basis. This can include strategy, planning, budgeting, team coordination, agency management, campaign oversight and performance reporting without immediately hiring a full-time senior Marketing Manager.

What is an outsourced marketing manager?

An outsourced marketing manager acts as an external marketing lead for the business and helps coordinate marketing strategy, internal staff, agencies, freelancers and other specialist resources.

Does SearchToLeads have a team to support execution?

SearchToLeads is consultant-led, with Chris Dona remaining the strategic point of contact. When additional execution capacity is required, specialist resources can be coordinated across areas such as creative design, content, video production, web development, paid media and other marketing disciplines depending on project scope.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on visibility in traditional search engines. AEO focuses on structuring useful content so answer engines can understand and surface relevant answers. GEO focuses on improving how brands and content can be discovered, understood and referenced within generative AI-search environments.

Does AEO or GEO replace SEO?

No. Technical SEO, useful content, search intent, site architecture, internal linking and authority remain important foundations. AEO and GEO extend these principles into newer answer-based and generative-search experiences.

Is outsourced marketing suitable for SMEs in Dubai?

It can be particularly useful for SMEs that need experienced marketing direction but do not yet require a large full-time marketing department. The model can combine senior marketing leadership with internal resources, existing agencies and external specialists.
