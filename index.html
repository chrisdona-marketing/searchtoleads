Frequently Asked Questions

SearchToLeads FAQs

Common questions about Fractional Marketing Management, SEO, AI search and growth marketing.

What is a Fractional Marketing Manager?

A Fractional Marketing Manager provides senior marketing leadership on a flexible basis. The role can include strategy, budget direction, channel planning, agency or vendor management, team coordination and performance reporting without requiring the business to immediately hire a full-time senior Marketing Manager.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on improving visibility in traditional search engines. AEO focuses on structuring content so answer engines can understand and surface useful answers. GEO focuses on improving how brands, expertise and content are discovered and interpreted by generative AI search systems.

Does AEO or GEO replace traditional SEO?

No. Technical SEO, useful content, site architecture, authority, internal linking and clear business information remain important. AEO and GEO extend those foundations for newer answer-based and generative-search experiences.

Is SearchToLeads suitable for SMEs in the UAE?

Yes. The service model is particularly relevant to SMEs and growing businesses that need structured marketing strategy, improved digital visibility and stronger lead-generation processes without necessarily building a large in-house team.

Can SearchToLeads manage both strategy and execution?

The approach combines strategic marketing leadership with practical digital expertise. Depending on the engagement, SearchToLeads can provide strategy, implementation support, performance oversight and coordination with internal teams, agencies or specialist partners.

How do you measure marketing performance?

Measurement should be aligned with the business objective. Depending on the engagement, this can include search visibility, qualified enquiries, conversion rates, paid-media efficiency, landing-page performance, engagement, channel contribution and other measurable acquisition indicators.
