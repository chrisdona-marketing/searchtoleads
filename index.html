What are SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on improving visibility in traditional search engines. AEO, or Answer Engine Optimization, focuses on structuring useful content so questions can be answered clearly. GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, focuses on making information easier for generative and AI-driven discovery systems to understand and potentially use. No approach can guarantee rankings or AI citations.
