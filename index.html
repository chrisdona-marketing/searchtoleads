Digital Marketing Consultant Dubai FAQs

What does a digital marketing consultant in Dubai do?

A digital marketing consultant helps businesses plan and improve marketing across SEO, paid media, content, websites, analytics and lead generation. Support can include strategy, channel planning, performance analysis and coordination with internal or external resources.

Do you offer freelance digital marketing consulting?

SearchToLeads offers independent marketing consulting that can be structured as project-based support, strategic advisory, outsourced marketing management or ongoing fractional marketing leadership.

What is a Fractional Marketing Manager?

A Fractional Marketing Manager provides experienced marketing leadership on a flexible basis without requiring a business to immediately hire a full-time senior Marketing Manager.

What is an outsourced marketing manager?

An outsourced marketing manager works as an external marketing lead and can coordinate internal employees, agencies, freelancers and specialist resources around the overall marketing strategy.

Does SearchToLeads provide execution support?

Yes, where required. SearchToLeads is consultant-led, with Chris Dona remaining the strategic point of contact. Additional specialist resources can be coordinated for creative design, content, video production, website development, paid media and other marketing activities depending on the project.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on traditional search-engine visibility. AEO focuses on helping answer engines understand and surface useful answers. GEO focuses on improving how brands and content can be discovered and interpreted by generative AI search systems.

Does AEO or GEO replace SEO?

No. Technical SEO, useful content, search intent, site structure, internal linking and authority remain important foundations. AEO and GEO extend those principles to newer answer-based and generative-search experiences.
