Frequently Asked Questions

Digital Marketing Consultant Dubai FAQs

Questions businesses commonly ask about marketing consultants, fractional marketing, outsourced marketing, SEO and AI-search optimisation.

What does a digital marketing consultant in Dubai do?

A digital marketing consultant can help assess and improve your digital marketing across SEO, paid advertising, content, websites, analytics and lead generation. The engagement can include strategy, channel planning, performance analysis and coordination with internal or external resources.

Do you offer freelance digital marketing consulting in Dubai?

SearchToLeads offers flexible independent marketing consultancy. Engagements can be structured as project-based consulting, strategic advisory, outsourced marketing management or ongoing fractional marketing leadership depending on your business requirement.

What is a Fractional Marketing Manager?

A Fractional Marketing Manager provides experienced marketing leadership on a flexible basis. Support can include strategy, budgeting, channel planning, agency management, team coordination, campaign oversight and performance reporting.

What is an outsourced marketing manager?

An outsourced marketing manager acts as an external marketing lead for the business and can coordinate internal staff, agencies, freelancers and specialist partners around the overall marketing strategy.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on traditional search-engine visibility. AEO focuses on helping answer engines understand and surface useful answers. GEO focuses on strengthening how brands and content can be discovered and interpreted by generative AI search systems.

Does AEO or GEO replace traditional SEO?

No. Technical SEO, useful content, search intent, internal linking, site structure and authority remain important foundations. AEO and GEO build on those foundations for newer search experiences.

Is outsourced marketing suitable for SMEs in Dubai?

It can be particularly useful for SMEs that need experienced marketing leadership but do not yet need a large full-time internal marketing department.
