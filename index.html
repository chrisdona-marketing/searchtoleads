Frequently Asked Questions

Digital Marketing Consultant Dubai FAQs

Common questions about digital marketing consulting, fractional marketing, outsourced marketing, SEO and AI-search optimisation.

What does a digital marketing consultant in Dubai do?

A digital marketing consultant helps businesses improve marketing strategy and execution across SEO, paid advertising, content, websites, analytics and lead generation. Support can include strategy, channel planning, performance analysis and coordination with internal and external resources.

Do you offer freelance digital marketing consulting in Dubai?

SearchToLeads provides independent digital marketing consulting. Support can be structured as project-based consulting, strategic advisory, outsourced marketing management or ongoing fractional marketing leadership.

What is a Fractional Marketing Manager?

A Fractional Marketing Manager provides experienced marketing leadership on a flexible basis. Support can include strategy, planning, budgeting, team coordination, agency management, campaign oversight and performance reporting.

What is an outsourced marketing manager?

An outsourced marketing manager acts as an external marketing lead and can coordinate internal staff, agencies, freelancers and specialist resources around the overall marketing strategy.

Does SearchToLeads provide additional execution support?

Yes, where required. SearchToLeads is consultant-led, with Chris Dona remaining the strategic point of contact. Additional specialist resources can be coordinated across creative design, content, video production, website development, paid media and other marketing activities depending on project scope.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO focuses on traditional search-engine visibility. AEO focuses on helping answer engines understand and surface useful answers. GEO focuses on improving how brands and content can be discovered and interpreted by generative AI-search systems.

Does AEO or GEO replace traditional SEO?

No. Technical SEO, useful content, search intent, site architecture, internal linking and authority remain important foundations. AEO and GEO extend these principles into newer answer-based and generative-search experiences.
